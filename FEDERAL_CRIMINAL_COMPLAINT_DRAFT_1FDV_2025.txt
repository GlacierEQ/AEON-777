*Richmond Newspapers, Inc. v. Virginia*, 448 U.S. 555, 580 (1980):

*Press-Enterprise Co. v. Superior Court*, 464 U.S. 501, 510 (1984):
- Test: "Experience and logic" test for right of access
- Historic: Family court proceedings traditionally accessible to public
- Presumption: Access presumed, denial requires justification

Hawaii Constitutional Standard:
- Hawaii Constitution Article I, Section 4: "Right of privacy recognized"
- Article I, Section 14: Right to open courts
- Analysis: Balance between privacy and transparency required
- Court failed to conduct balancing test
