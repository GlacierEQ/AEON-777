## PART 1: TRO ISSUANCE & VALIDITY ANALYSIS

### A. HRS §580-1 Requirements for TRO Issuance

#### Statutory Framework
Hawaii Revised Statutes §580-1(a) permits temporary restraining orders in family court only when:
1. ✅ Valid family law case exists
2. ⚠️ "Good cause" exists for immediate issuance
3. ⚠️ Plaintiff demonstrates likelihood of irreparable harm
4. ⚠️ Balance of equities favors restraint
5. ❌ **Plaintiff provides written notice to defendant** (HFCR 65)

#### Status in 1FDV-23-0001009
| Requirement | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Valid case | ✅ YES | Case active since 2023 |
| Good cause | ❌ NO | Voicemail-only notification (Dkt #203) fails requirement |
| Irreparable harm | ⚠️ QUESTIONABLE | Affidavit not provided in docket |
| Balance of equities | ❌ NO | Court favored plaintiff unilaterally; no balanced hearing |
| **Notice requirement** | ❌ **CRITICAL FAILURE** | Voicemail after 1:31 PM start time; no certified mail; no JEFS confirmation |

### B. Notice Defect — Fatal Blow to TRO Validity

#### Timeline of Alleged "Notice"
- **Hearing scheduled:** June 19, 2025, 1:31 PM
- **Calls placed:** Three simultaneous calls at 1:31 PM (during hearing start)
- **Pattern:** No calls at 1:00 PM, 12:30 PM, or 1:15 PM
- **Affidavit:** NONE provided to prove:
  - Correct phone number used
  - Casey actually called
  - Casey actually received message
  - Timing of voicemail deposit

#### Legal Standard — Mathews v. Eldridge
*Mathews v. Eldridge*, 424 U.S. 319 (1976) requires notice "calculated to apprise [the defendant] of the pendency of [the] action and to enable him to present his defense."

**Hawaii Application:** *Citibank v. O'Neill*, 79 Haw. 172 — Notice by voicemail alone is **insufficient** when formal service methods available.

#### Finding
🚨 **TRO is VOID AB INITIO** due to inadequate notice.
