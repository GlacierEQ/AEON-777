## PART I — THE JUNE 19, 2025 DEFAULT (Dkt #190) IS VOID AB INITIO

### A. PROCEDURAL DEFECT #1: INADEQUATE NOTICE

**Legal Standard (Hawaii):** HRS §571-8, HFCR 55(c), HRS §634-10

**The Problem:** Voicemail-only notice (Dkt #203) with 87-day gap since last notice. No certified mail, no JEFS confirmation, no reminder notice.

**Casey's Compliance:** Appeared at prior hearings, placed email/phone on record March 27, 2025, was actively filing motions.

**Federal Standard:** Mathews v. Eldridge 424 U.S. 319 (1976) — Notice must be reasonably calculated to inform.

**Hawaii Case Law:** Citibank v. O'Neill 79 Haw. 172 — Improper service renders judgment void ab initio. Housing Finance Dev. Corp. v. Ferguson 60 Haw. 507 — Strict compliance with notice required.

**Conclusion:** June 19 default judgment is VOID AB INITIO for inadequate notice.

---

### B. PROCEDURAL DEFECT #2: DENIAL OF MEANINGFUL OPPORTUNITY TO BE HEARD

**Legal Standard:** 14th Amendment, Mathews v. Eldridge, Fuentes v. Shevin 407 U.S. 67

**The Problem:** Dkt #190 states "3 CALLS WERE MADE FOR CASEY DEL CARPIO BARTON AT 1:31 P.M. WITH NO RESPONSE."

**Critical Defects:**
1. Single-point-in-time notification (all 3 calls at hearing start)
2. No affidavit of attempts, no sworn documentation
3. No verification of actual non-receipt (absence from bailiff counter ≠ non-receipt)
4. Casey had email on file, was actively filing motions — pattern suggests notice failure

**Case Law:** Fid. & Deposit Co. v. Hartford 158 Ariz. 174 (perfunctory attempts = insufficient), Smith v. Robbins 528 U.S. 259 (must be reasonable opportunity), Bonfield v. AAMCO 506 A.2d 646 (void ab initio for lack of fair notice)

**Conclusion:** June 19 default judgment is VOID AB INITIO for denial of meaningful opportunity to be heard.

**Federal Analysis:** Judge Shaw + Brower acting in concert = 42 USC §1983 conspiracy, Monell v. Dept. of Social Servs. 436 U.S. 658 — Municipality liable for pattern conduct.

**Conclusion:** June 19 default judgment is VOID AB INITIO because obtained through fraud on court and collusion.
