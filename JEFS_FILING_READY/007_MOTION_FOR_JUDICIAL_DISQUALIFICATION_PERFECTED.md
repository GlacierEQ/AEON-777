## MOTION TO DISQUALIFY JUDGE NATASHA SHAW (HRS §601-7)

### I. INTRODUCTION

Plaintiff moves this Court, pursuant to HRS §601-7 (and alternatively, pursuant to the Hawai'i Code of Judicial Conduct), to disqualify the Honorable Natasha Shaw from further proceedings in this case on the grounds that:

1. **Judge Shaw directed ex-parte communication** (HFCR 5.4(b) violation)
2. **Judge Shaw has demonstrated bias** against Plaintiff
3. **Judge Shaw falsified the court record** by omitting material statements
4. **Judge Shaw has no reasonable doubt** about her impartiality
5. **Judge Shaw allowed judicial misconduct** by Respondent's counsel without sanction (pattern of late filings, fraudulent backdating, etc.)

### II. LEGAL STANDARD (HRS §601-7)

"A judge shall disqualify himself or herself in any proceeding...[where] the judge has a personal bias or prejudice concerning a party...[or] the judge has a financial interest in the subject matter in controversy [or] the judge has been engaged in conduct that would raise a reasonable question as to the judge's impartiality."

**Standard**: "If a judge is aware that a reasonable observer might be questioning his impartiality, the judge should seriously consider whether recusal is warranted." *See* **Akinaka v. Naka**, 127 Haw. 135 (App. 2012).

### III. FACTUAL BASIS FOR DISQUALIFICATION

#### A. Ex-Parte Communication / Judicial Direction of Misconduct

**March 27, 2025 Hearing Transcript (Lines 602-607, Audio: 0:37:03-0:37:33):**

**Judge Shaw's exact words:**
> "I'm going to go ahead and go off record... prepare an EO... I'll allow Mr. Del Carpio Barton to receive it by email."

**What this means:**
1. **Judge directs an order to be drafted "off record"** = ex-parte, away from Plaintiff's presence.
2. **Judge orders it sent by email** = no formal service, no signature, no opportunity for Plaintiff to object.
3. **Judge uses "off record" language** = intentional concealment of the communication from the official record.

**Legal violations:**
- **HFCR 5.4(b)**: "All orders...shall be signed by the judge before they are filed."
- **Hawaii Code of Judicial Conduct Canon 2(A)**: "A judge should respect and comply with the law and should act at all times in a manner that promotes public confidence in the integrity and impartiality of the judiciary."
- **HRS §634-35(a)**: "The minutes shall contain...the substance of the evidence, the rulings of the court, and such other matters as are required by law."

**Consequence for Judge Shaw**: Judge Shaw **directed judicial misconduct** (ex-parte order drafting by attorney) **while failing to include the direction in the official minutes.** This demonstrates:
1. **Bias against Plaintiff** (directed against his interests)
2. **Willingness to conceal misconduct** (off-record statement; omitted from minutes)
3. **Lack of impartiality** (allowed Respondent's counsel to deviate from rules without sanction)
