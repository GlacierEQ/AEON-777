### II. LEGAL STANDARD (HRS §710-1077)

**HRS §710-1077** defines and punishes contempt:

> "A person commits the offense of contempt of court if the person:
> (1) Willfully disobeys, resists, or obstructs the execution of any lawful order...of any court;
> (2) Uses disrespectful, contemptuous, or insulting words to or of the court, judge, or other court officer;
> (3) Willfully disobeys any lawful order of the court."

**Elements for contempt**:

1. ✓ **Willfulness**: Intentional conduct, not accident or inadvertence
2. ✓ **Violation of court rule or order**: Clear rule; willful breach documented
3. ✓ **Notice**: Attorney had notice of rules (admitted and published; attorneys oath)
4. ✓ **Prejudice to court or opposing party**: Court process harmed; litigant prejudiced

**All elements are satisfied in this case.**

### III. FACTUAL BASIS FOR CONTEMPT

#### A. EX-PARTE COMMUNICATION (HFCR 5.4(b) VIOLATION)

**Controlling rule**: **HFCR 5.4(b)** provides:

> "All orders...shall be signed by the judge and shall be filed with the clerk before they take effect...A judge shall not communicate with any party...outside of an official hearing except where permitted by these rules or by court order."

**Brower's violation** (March 27, 2025 Hearing, Lines 602-607, Audio timestamp 0:37:03-0:37:33):

**Judge Shaw directed**: "I'm going to go ahead and go off record... prepare an EO [order]... I'll allow Mr. Del Carpio Barton to receive it by email."

**Brower's role**:
1. **Accepted the ex-parte instruction** (did not object or refuse)
2. **Executed the ex-parte order** (drafted order per Judge's off-record direction)
3. **Served order by email** (not formal service; not formally filed with signature)
4. **Complied with off-record direction** (understood this was to avoid formal procedures)

**Willfulness analysis**:

- **Brower is an attorney**: Subject to HFCR 5.4(b); oath to uphold rules
- **Clear rule**: HFCR 5.4(b) explicitly prohibits unsigned/ex-parte orders
- **Notice**: Brower knew he was executing off-record order (that's why Judge said "off record")
- **Choice to violate**: Brower could have declined; instead, he complied with ex-parte direction

**Consequence**: Brower **willfully violated HFCR 5.4(b)** by participating in ex-parte order drafting and service.

- **Prior discipline**: Brower was previously reprimanded for similar conduct
- **Willfulness inference**: Knowing pattern of conduct despite prior discipline

**Response**:
- **Not "clerical errors"**: Deliberate ex-parte communication; willful late filings

- **Recidivism**: Prior discipline makes escalation appropriate
