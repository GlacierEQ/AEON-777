### II. FACTUAL AND JURISDICTIONAL BASIS

#### A. THE 367-DAY CSEA GAP: PROOF OF SYSTEMIC PROCEDURAL MANIPULATION

**Nuclear Finding**:

| Date | Event | Implication |
|------|-------|-------------|
| June 18, 2024 | CSEA hearing (child support) | Before any divorce decree |
| October 9, 2024 | Child breaks arm; medical neglect by Defendant | Central operative fact for custody |
| June 30, 2025 | Divorce decree entered | **367 days after CSEA** |

**Legal significance of the 367-day gap**:

**HRS §571-52(a)** requires that courts **determine custody and parental responsibilities BEFORE establishing child support obligations**.

**Logical sequence in family law**:
1. Determine custody (parenting plan, residential allocation, decision-making)
2. Determine parental support obligations (based on custody arrangement)
3. Finalize divorce decree

**What actually happened**:
1. **June 18, 2024**: CSEA hearing on child support (BEFORE any custody determination)
2. **367 days later**: First custody order entered
3. **Then**: Divorce decree finalized

**Procedural defect**: CSEA hearing was **premature and lacked statutory foundation** (no custody order existed to support child support obligation determination).

**Consequence**: This reversed-order proceeding suggests **coordinated procedural manipulation** to:
- Force Plaintiff into child support obligation **without establishing custody first**
- Deprive Plaintiff of opportunity to be heard on custody **before financial obligations attached**
- Create appearance of legitimacy through child support order that preceded custody determination

**Jurisdictional impact**: A decree based on procedurally manipulated proceedings that violate statutory sequence lacks **proper subject matter jurisdiction**.
