### II. LEGAL STANDARD (HRS §710-1077)

"A person commits the offense of contempt of court if the person willfully disobeys, resists, or obstructs the execution of any lawful order...of any court...[or] uses disrespectful, contemptuous, or insulting words to or of the court, judge, or other court officer...[or] willfully disobeys any lawful order of the court."

**Elements for contempt**:
1. **Willfulness** (intentional conduct, not accident)
2. **Violation of court rules** (clear rule; willful breach)
3. **Civil or criminal sanctions available** (depending on nature of violation)
4. **Notice** (attorney had notice of rules)

**All elements are satisfied.**

### III. FACTUAL BASIS FOR CONTEMPT

#### A. Ex-Parte Communication (HFCR 5.4(b) Violation)

**HFCR 5.4(b)**: "All orders...shall be signed by the judge and shall be filed with the clerk before they take effect...A judge shall not communicate with any party...outside of an official hearing except where permitted by these rules or by court order."

**Brower's violation** (March 27, 2025 Hearing, Lines 602-607):

**Judge Shaw directed**: "I'm going to go ahead and go off record... prepare an EO [order]... I'll allow Mr. Del Carpio Barton to receive it by email."

**What happened**:
1. Judge told Brower to draft an order **off the record** (outside Plaintiff's presence and knowledge)
2. Judge directed order be sent **by email** (not through formal service)
3. Judge did **not sign it formally** and directed Brower to handle service
4. Order was **never filed with the clerk** with a formal signature block

**Brower's role**: 
- **Accepted the ex-parte instruction** (did not object)
- **Executed the ex-parte order** (drafted and sent by email)
- **Violated HFCR 5.4(b)** by accepting an order that would not be formally signed/filed

**Contempt**: Brower **willfully violated HFCR 5.4(b)** by participating in ex-parte order drafting and service.
