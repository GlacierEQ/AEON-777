## MOTION PORTFOLIO (Strategic Filing Order)

### PHASE 1: EMERGENCY RELIEF (File First)

**001 — Motion to Stay Proceedings (HFCR 62(a))**
- **Purpose**: Halt all proceedings pending appeal; suspend custody orders
- **Standard**: Substantial legal questions + irreparable harm + balance of hardships
- **Key Claims**: Constitutional due process violation; jurisdictional defect; record falsification
- **Relief**: Stay all proceedings; restore status quo (parental contact)

**003 — Writ of Habeas Corpus (HRS §660-1)**
- **Purpose**: Challenge unlawful custody detention at appellate level
- **Jurisdiction**: Hawaii Supreme Court (original jurisdiction)
- **Standard**: Illegal restraint + no adequate remedy at law
- **Key Claims**: Void custody order; mandatory statutory violations; ongoing physical harm risk
- **Relief**: Issue writ; restore custody pending evidentiary hearing

**002 — Motion to Vacate Custody Order (HFCR 60(b)(4))**
- **Purpose**: Void custody order for lack of statutory/constitutional compliance
- **Standard**: Void ab initio (lack of jurisdiction, due process violation)
- **Key Claims**: No parenting plan; no psychological evaluation; no hearing; documented neglect
- **Relief**: Vacate entire custody order; restore custody; order psychological evaluation
