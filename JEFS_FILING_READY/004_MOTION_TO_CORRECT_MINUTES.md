### III. LEGAL STANDARD

#### A. HFCR 80(a): Court's Authority to Amend Minutes

"The court shall use reasonable diligence in the preparation of its findings and conclusions...During the pendency of any case or proceeding before the court, the court, either of its own motion or upon the motion of a party, shall have the authority to correct and amend any order, judgment, or minutes that contain errors of substance."

**Standard**: "Errors of substance" that affect the record may be corrected at any time before appeal.

#### B. HRS §634-35(a): Clerk's Duty

"The clerk shall keep a record of the proceedings of the court...written in a clear and legible manner...The minutes shall contain...the substance of the evidence, the rulings of the court, and such other matters as are required by law."

**"Substance of the evidence"** clearly includes Plaintiff's jurisdiction objection and the Judge's ex-parte instruction.

#### C. Due Process Dimension

Accurate court minutes are **essential to due process:**
- **Brady v. Maryland**, 373 U.S. 83 (1963): Suppression of material evidence violates due process.
- **Falsified minutes = suppression of material evidence on the record** → Due process violation.
