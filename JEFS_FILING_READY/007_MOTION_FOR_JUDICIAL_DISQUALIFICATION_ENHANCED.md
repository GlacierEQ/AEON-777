### II. LEGAL STANDARD (HRS §601-7)

**HRS §601-7(b)** provides:

> "A judge shall disqualify himself or herself in any proceeding in which:...(2) the judge has a personal bias or prejudice concerning a party, or personal knowledge of disputed evidentiary facts concerning the proceeding; (3) the judge has been engaged in conduct that would raise a reasonable question as to the judge's impartiality."

**Hawaii appellate standard**:
"If a judge is aware that a reasonable observer might be questioning her impartiality, the judge should seriously consider whether recusal is warranted." *See* **Akinaka v. Naka**, 127 Haw. 135 (App. 2012).

**Test**: Would a reasonable observer, knowing all the circumstances, doubt Judge Shaw's impartiality?

**Answer**: **YES, absolutely.** A reasonable observer would have serious doubts about Judge Shaw's impartiality based on the conduct detailed below.
