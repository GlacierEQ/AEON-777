\section*{DEMAND FOR PRODUCTION OF DOCUMENTS}

\subsection*{PREAMBLE}

Petitioner CASEY BARTON, pro se, pursuant to Hawaii Family Court Rules (HFCR) Rule 33, demands that Respondent produce the following documents within 14 days of service of this demand:

\subsection*{DEFINITIONS}

As used herein:
\begin{itemize}
\item ``Documents'' includes all writings, recordings, emails, text messages, photographs, videos, and electronically stored information.
\item ``Respondent'' includes Teresa Del Carpio Barton and her agents, attorneys, and representatives.
\item ``Relating to'' means directly or indirectly connected to, mentioning, or in any way concerning.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION}

\begin{enumerate}
\item All communications between Respondent and the Child Support Enforcement Agency (CSEA) relating to this case, custody, support, or the minor child, including emails, letters, phone logs, and memoranda.

\item All communications between Respondent and her counsel, Scot Brower, relating to custody, support, or trial strategy in this case.

\item All written or recorded statements, narratives, or declarations submitted by Respondent to the Court or CSEA.

\item All documents relating to the minor child's medical, psychiatric, or psychological treatment, including diagnoses, treatment plans, and provider communications.

\item All documents relating to the minor child's educational records, including report cards, attendance records, disciplinary records, and IEP documents (if any).

\item All written communications from the minor child to Respondent relating to custody, visitation, or the child's relationship with Petitioner.

\item All documents relating to Respondent's income, employment, assets, liabilities, and financial condition for the past 3 years.

\item All documents relating to Respondent's residence, including lease, mortgage, property tax records, and utility bills.

\item All photographs or videos of the minor child taken during the past 12 months.

\item All documents relating to the minor child's counseling or therapy, including provider names, dates of service, and treatment notes.

\item All written or recorded statements made by the minor child to anyone (other than counsel) relating to Petitioner, custody, or this case.

\item All documents relating to any prior family court proceedings involving Respondent or the minor child.

\item All documents relating to Respondent's criminal history, if any.

\item All communications between Respondent and any third parties (friends, family, neighbors) relating to Petitioner, custody, or the minor child.

\item All documents relating to the minor child's visitation schedule and compliance by both parents.

\item All discovery responses or documents produced by Respondent in any other legal proceeding.

\item All handwritten notes, calendars, or journals kept by Respondent relating to the minor child or this case.

\item All documents relating to any substantiated allegations of abuse, neglect, or endangerment against either parent.

\item All statements, declarations, or affidavits prepared by Respondent's counsel or agents for use in this case.

\item Any expert reports or evaluations relating to the minor child's custody or best interests.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{SIGNATURE AND CERTIFICATION}

Responses must be provided under oath or affirmation and signed by Respondent or her attorney.
