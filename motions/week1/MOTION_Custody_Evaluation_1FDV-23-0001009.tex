\section*{MOTION FOR CHILD CUSTODY EVALUATION}

\subsection*{I. INTRODUCTION}

Petitioner CASEY BARTON, pro se, respectfully moves this Honorable Court for an Order requiring a comprehensive psychosocial custody evaluation of the minor child, KEKOA BARTON, pursuant to Hawaii Revised Statutes \S~571-46(b) and Rule 16 of the Hawaii Family Court Rules.

\subsection*{II. FACTUAL BASIS}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The minor child, KEKOA BARTON (age 14), is the subject of a custody determination in this Court.

\item The child has been diagnosed with clinical depression and is currently in psychological treatment.

\item The child's best interests cannot be reliably determined without a comprehensive psychosocial assessment.

\item HRS \S~571-46(b) authorizes the Court to order psychiatric, psychological, or other evaluations to determine custody, guardianship, or support arrangements in the best interests of the child.

\item A qualified evaluator (licensed psychologist or psychiatrist) would assess:
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item The child's adjustment to each parent and home environment
  \item The child's emotional and psychological needs
  \item Each parent's capacity to meet the child's physical and emotional needs
  \item The child's expressed preferences regarding custody
  \item The quality and stability of the child's relationship with each parent
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{III. LEGAL STANDARD}

Under HRS \S~571-46(b), courts have broad discretion to order evaluations when evaluations ``assist in determining'' matters affecting the child's welfare. This case presents classic indicators for evaluation:
\begin{itemize}
\item Contested custody matter
\item Child with documented mental health condition
\item Significant parental conflict
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{IV. CONCLUSION}

For the foregoing reasons, Petitioner respectfully requests that this Court order a comprehensive psychosocial custody evaluation of the minor child, KEKOA BARTON, at Respondent's expense.
