\section*{MOTION FOR GUARDIANSHIP AD LITEM APPOINTMENT}

\subsection*{I. INTRODUCTION}

Petitioner CASEY BARTON, pro se, respectfully moves this Honorable Court pursuant to Hawaii Revised Statutes \S~571-46(a)(1) for the appointment of a Guardian Ad Litem (``GAL'') to represent the best interests of the minor child, KEKOA BARTON, in this custody matter. This motion is filed based on the grounds and allegations set forth below:

\subsection*{II. FACTS AND LEGAL BASIS}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The minor child, KEKOA BARTON (age 14), is the subject of ongoing custody proceedings in this Court under Case No. 1FDV-23-0001009.

\item The Respondent has taken no meaningful steps to prioritize the child's stated preferences, counseling needs, or independent advocacy in this matter.

\item HRS \S~571-46(a)(1) expressly authorizes this Court to appoint a GAL ``in any case'' to represent the best interests of a child when the Court determines such representation is necessary.

\item The appointment of an independent GAL would serve the manifest interests of justice and the child's welfare by:
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item Providing an independent voice separate from parental litigation strategies
  \item Conducting an unbiased assessment of the child's preferences, needs, and best interests
  \item Documenting the child's emotional and psychological state
  \item Advising the Court on constructive custody arrangements
  \end{enumerate}

\item The child's emotional well-being is demonstrably at risk in the current adversarial climate.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{III. CONCLUSION}

For the foregoing reasons, Petitioner respectfully requests that this Court appoint a qualified Guardian Ad Litem to represent the best interests of the minor child, KEKOA BARTON, in these proceedings.
