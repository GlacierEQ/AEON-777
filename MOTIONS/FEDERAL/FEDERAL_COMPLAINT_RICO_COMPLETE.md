## III. DETAILED PREDICATE ACTS & RACKETEERING

### **Predicate Act 1: Mail Fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1341)** — Judge Shaw

**Conduct**: Default entry backdating  
**Mailed Matter**: 
- JEFS electronic docket entries (transmitted via interstate internet/mail system)
- Default minute entry mailed to Plaintiff's address (AUG 16, 2023)
- Notice of default transmitted via Hawaii state court email system (interstate)

**Scheme to Defraud**: 
- Created fraudulent minute entry showing default entry at 12:08 PM while status conference ongoing
- Backdated entry to appear contemporaneous with hearing
- Fraudulent entry became predicate for 105 days of post-default orders + final decree

**Materiality**: Plaintiff relied on default entry's authenticity when failing to respond (no legal duty to respond to void default).

**Damages**: $2,585,000 (full Shaw liability from § 1983 complaint)

**Wire Elements**:

**Materiality**: Plaintiff relied on CSEA notices as authentic government documents; incurred legal costs + compliance burden.

**Damages**: $1,100,000 (CSEA liability from § 1983 complaint)
