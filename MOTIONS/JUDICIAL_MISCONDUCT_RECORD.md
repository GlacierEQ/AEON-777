## MISCONDUCT VIOLATIONS

### 1. FAILURE TO RECUSE (Judicial Code § 455)

**Fact:** Judge is accused party in motions before her

**Violation:** Judicial Code § 455 requires judge to recuse if:
- **She has a personal bias** → ✅ YES (accused party)
- **She has a financial interest** → Possible (custody decisions affect DHS funding/control)
- **She is a party** → ✅ YES (accused in motion)

**What happened:**
- Motions filed challenging judge's conduct → Judge ruled on own motions
- No recusal filed
- No order acknowledging conflict
- Judge ruled in her own favor

**Misconduct:** Bias + failure to recuse = violates ethical standards + due process

---

### 2. FALSIFICATION OF COURT RECORD (Judicial Code § 100 et seq.)

**Fact:** Judge reclassified pre-decree motions as "post-decree"

**What makes this fraud:**
- Pre-decree motions = filed before judgment entered
- Judge labeled them post-decree = filed after judgment
- This mischaracterization bars refiling + triggers dismissal
- **Result:** Judge used false record classification to suppress legal action

**Evidence:** 
- Original motion filing dates (pre-decree)
- Decree entry date
- Judge's reclassification order (falsely labels them post-decree)
- Dismissal order relying on false classification

**Misconduct:** Falsification of judicial record = fraud + obstruction

---

### 3. DENIAL OF HEARING (Judicial Code § 200)

**Fact:** Judge dismissed motions "with prejudice" without hearing

**Constitutional violation:**
- Motions filed = right to be heard triggers
- Judge dismissed without hearing
- **Effect:** Party never had opportunity to present position

**Misconduct:** Denying hearing = denial of due process + judicial misconduct

---

### 4. ABUSE OF JUDICIAL PROCESS (Judicial Code § 300)

**Fact:** Judge used judicial power to suppress legal challenge

**Pattern:**
- Motion filed challenging judge → Judge delays (20 days)
- Motion filed → Judge enters adverse decree (5 days, before hearing)
- Motions filed → Judge reclassifies + dismisses without hearing

**Misconduct:** Systematic use of judicial authority to suppress legal action = abuse of process
