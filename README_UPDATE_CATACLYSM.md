1. Actor had authority (judge presiding)
2. Act deprived constitutional right (due process + Brady)
3. Deprivation was intentional (knowing false record)
4. Proximate causation (false record caused custody deprivation)
5. Damages (lost custody + reputational harm)

### Pattern & Practice (§ 1985)

1. Conspiracy (judge + attorney coordinated)

3. Use of force/intimidation (threats against Kekoa + counsel)
4. Affecting interstate commerce (federal question)

### Brady/Giglio Suppression
**Elements**:
1. Prosecution (attorney in family court) withheld evidence
2. Evidence favorable to accused (audio vs. false minutes)
3. Materiality (evidence could affect outcome)

**Precedent**: Brady v. Maryland, Giglio v. United States

3. Systemic pattern (not isolated incident)

**Precedent**: CEDAW General Recommendation 33
