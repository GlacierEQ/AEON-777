# 🇺🇸 IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
**FOR THE DISTRICT OF HAWAII**

**CASEY BARTON**, Plaintiff,
v.
**THE HONORABLE NATASHA SHAW, et al.**, Defendants.

## COMPLAINT FOR VIOLATION OF CIVIL RIGHTS (42 U.S.C. § 1983)

### I. JURISDICTION AND VENUE
This Court has subject matter jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1331 and § 1343 over Plaintiff’s claims arising under the United States Constitution and 42 U.S.C. § 1983.

### II. NATURE OF THE ACTION
This is a civil rights action challenging the systemic deprivation of Plaintiff's fundamental right to familial association and due process under the Fourteenth Amendment, executed under color of state law by officers of the First Circuit Family Court of Hawaii.

### III. FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS
1. **Denial of Due Process**: Defendants have willfully refused to adjudicate Plaintiff's Motion for Reunification for over 380 days, effectively severing parental rights without a hearing.
2. **State-Created Danger**: Despite being presented with forensic audio evidence of the minor child suffering a concealed bone fracture (Physical Neglect), Defendants maintained orders keeping the child in the negligent environment.
3. **Obstruction of Procedural Access**: Plaintiff filed a **Motion to Stay** (STA) which has been willfully ignored by the Defendants for 20 consecutive days, effectively blocking Plaintiff's access to appellate remedies and further entrenching the deprivation of rights.
4. **Collusion & Fraud**: Officers of the court engaged in ex-parte communications and accepted fraudulent, backdated digital filings (April 2025) to justify unlawful rulings.
5. **Racketeering Enterprise**: Defendants participated in a coordinated scheme of mail/wire fraud (billing manipulation) and evidence tampering (Kokua purging) for the economic benefit of the collective enterprise (Greg Ryan Law / Yamamoto Trust), violating 18 U.S.C. § 1961 (RICO).

### IV. CAUSES OF ACTION
- **Count I**: Violation of Procedural Due Process (14th Amendment).
- **Count II**: Violation of Substantive Due Process (14th Amendment - Right to Family).

### V. PRAYER FOR RELIEF
Plaintiff seeks immediate injunctive relief halting state proceedings, declaratory judgment, and compensatory damages.
