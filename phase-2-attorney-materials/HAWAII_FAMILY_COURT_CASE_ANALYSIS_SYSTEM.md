## APPLICABLE HAWAII STATUTES

### HRS § 350 - Child Abuse Reporting Law

**Definition:** "Acts or omissions of any person responsible for child's care resulting in physical or psychological harm to child under 18."

**Specific Indicators (HRS 350-1(A)) - Direct Match to Your Case:**
- Substantial or multiple skin bruising or internal bleeding
- Injury to skin causing substantial bleeding
- Fractures
- Subdural hematoma
- Soft tissue swelling
- Extreme pain

**Key Principle:** "When injury is not justifiably explained, OR history given is at variance with injury type/degree, OR circumstances indicate injury may not be accidental"

### HRS § 587A - CHILD PROTECTIVE ACT (Family Court)

**Key Sections:**

| Section | Application |
|---------|----------|
| **587A-7** | Safe family home analysis; burden shifts to defendant once imminent harm shown |
| **587A-21** | Lower evidence threshold; hearsay admissible; child testimony admissible |
| **587A-25** | Preponderance of evidence standard (51%, not criminal 99%) |
| **587A-38** | Protective order if "necessary to prevent domestic abuse or recurrence" |

**Your Advantage:** Pattern evidence (two injuries) directly supports "imminent harm" + "recurrence" findings.
